OPORD Arma 3 multiplayer scenario Altis Vanilla + CBA Co-op, up to 26 players

ComQuest

A CSAT carrier parks off Altis and starts planting headquarters inland. Each one grows, splits and feeds the next. Your job is to find the network and cut it apart before it reaches your airfield.

01
The enemy

A network that grows on its own

Nothing is placed by hand. A server-side director runs the enemy as a living system: it inserts, builds, resupplies, redeploys and calls for help, every few seconds, whether you are watching or not.

  1. STEP 1

    Insertion

    Two minutes in, Taru and Mohawk transports leave the carrier. The first HQ is a beachhead, inside 2 km of the shore.

  2. STEP 2

    Paradrop

    A Zamak and an Ifrit come down under their own chutes, at least 350 m from the water.

  3. STEP 3

    Secure

    Squads post out to a 1 km perimeter before anyone lays a sandbag.

  4. STAGE 1/4

    Command post

    The HQ commander moves in. Kill or capture him: he matters.

  5. STAGE 2/4

    Perimeter

    Sandbags around the site.

  6. STAGE 3/4

    Depot and radio

    Ammo depot, fuel tanker, supply truck and a radio mast.

  7. STAGE 4/4

    Operational

    Helipad with a QRF helicopter, three AA pits, an HMG tower to the west. Two squads of armed locals join them.

  8. THEN

    Split

    Once it is strong enough, the HQ sends colonists out by truck and helicopter to found the next one.

Logistics

The main HQ receives ammunition by air. HEMTT convoys carry it on to the others. Every new HQ starts short of ammo, so a dead convoy is felt across the island.

Redeployment

Every minute the director evens out the infantry over the built HQs. Squads move by truck or helicopter when one is within 200 m. One squad always walks.

Movement

Ifrits, Hunters and armour roam between HQs and avoid hilltops and rock fields. Orca and Kajman helicopters fly irregular patrols over the whole network.

Support teams

A medic team in a white Strider keeps the wounded alive. An engineer team repairs damaged vehicles. Each is dropped 40 s after the first insertion and comes back 15 min after it dies.

Reinforcements

Weakened HQs call in fresh sections from the carrier, sent straight to the HQs that need them, including ones still being built.

No markers

Enemy positions never appear on your map. You find them with the radio direction finder, or by making an HQ commander talk.

02
Contact

Get spotted and it escalates

Each HQ reacts to contact on its own, in steps. How fast you kill the squad that spotted you decides how bad it gets.

CONTACT

The spotting squad engages and calls on every patrolling squad within 700 m to join in.

RED FLARE

If that squad loses 40% of its men within two minutes, a red flare goes up over the HQ. Heavy support follows: 30% of the garrison and half of the roaming squads within 2.5 km come back to defend.

ROOT DEFENCE

Attack the first HQ, the root of the network, and every HQ it founded sends troops back to defend it.

03
Win and lose

Victory is total, defeat is one man

An HQ falls

Only when its commander is dead or captured and every installation is destroyed: depot, fuel, radio mast, helipad and AA.

Capture the commander

Hold the action on him. He talks: every HQ in his network goes on your map.

Victory

Every enemy HQ on Altis destroyed.

Defeat

Colonel Harlan, the friendly commander in your HQ offices, is killed. Then the operation is over.

04
Your base

An airfield compound and its motor pool

The base is built by script at the AAC airfield, from zones drawn on the map: a walled HQ, four vehicle rows, crate dispensers, repair depots facing the runway, and a vehicle shop.

HQ compound

H-barrier walls, a vehicle gate between two towers, guard towers on the corners. Inside: warehouse, HQ offices, cargo HQ, radar, medical post, and one equipment box per squad with a full arsenal.

Vehicle rows

Four parallel rows. Vehicles park side by side, at right angles to the row, smallest first. The gap is each model's width plus 5 m. Every row gets a similar mix, so every squad has a motor pool.

Crates and loadout

Each row ends at a dispenser with ready-filled supply crates you can load into any vehicle, and a loadout zone where one action fills a vehicle with resupply for a whole squad.

Repair and defence

Two repair depots facing the runway repair, refuel and rearm anything that stops there. AA and HMG positions around the perimeter are rebuilt 10 minutes after they fall.

06
Intel

Three voices on the net, no coordinates

Messages never give a location. Intel stays thin on purpose: building progress, supplies received, reactions to what you just destroyed, and alarms. The tone is serious milsim with the dry humour of an old staff officer.

Who is talking

CROSSROAD is your HQ. MAGPIE is the drone operator and reports facts only. INTERCEPT is translated enemy radio, heard only while that HQ's radio mast stands.

Direction finder

SIGNAL65 %

Carry a radio and a panel at the top of the screen picks up the closest fully built HQ within 6 km. The antenna is directional: sweep your view to find the bearing, walk in to raise the level.

08
Under the hood

Vanilla + CBA

Base-game assets only. Every class is checked for DLC at runtime and falls back to a base-game one when needed.

One director

The server runs a single loop with self-timed tasks: HQs, logistics, air, balancing, roaming, crates, repair, stats.

Tunable

Timings, limits, prices, layout and the radio script live in two files: cfg/settings.sqf and cfg/story.sqf.
