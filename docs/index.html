01
The enemy

A network that grows on its own

Nothing is placed by hand. A server-side director runs the enemy as a living system: it inserts, builds, resupplies, redeploys and calls for help every few seconds, whether you are watching or not. The carrier holds enough for six HQs, and its marker shows what is left.

Step 1

Insertion

Taru and Mohawk transports leave the carrier. The first HQ is a beachhead within 2 km of the shore.

Step 2

Paradrop

A Zamak and an Ifrit come down under their own chutes, plus a long-range Tigris anti-air vehicle with every insertion.

Step 3

Secure

Squads post out to a 1 km perimeter before anyone lays a sandbag.

Then

Split

A strong HQ sends colonists by truck and helicopter to found the next one, until the carrier is empty.

Stage 1/4

Command post

The HQ commander moves in. Kill or capture him: he matters.

Stage 2/4

Perimeter

Sandbags around the site.

Stage 3/4

Depot and radio

Ammo depot, fuel tanker, supply truck and a radio tower.

Stage 4/4

Operational

Helipad with a QRF helicopter, three AA pits, an HMG tower, and two squads of armed locals.

Logistics

The main HQ is resupplied by air and HEMTT convoys carry ammunition on to the others. A dead convoy is felt across the island.

Redeployment

Every minute the director evens out infantry across built HQs, by truck or helicopter. At most 160 enemy soldiers are on the ground at once.

Movement

Ifrits, Hunters and armour roam between HQs and avoid hilltops. Orca and Kajman helicopters fly irregular patrols.

Support teams

A medic team and an engineer team keep the network running. Each comes back three times, then it is gone for good.

Reinforcements

Weakened HQs call fresh sections from the carrier, while it still has men to send.

Rule

No markers

Enemy positions never appear on your map by themselves. Drones, the direction finder or a talkative commander put them there.

02
Contact

Get spotted and it escalates

Each HQ reacts to contact on its own, in steps. How fast you kill the squad that spotted you decides how bad it gets.

CONTACT

The spotting squad engages and calls every patrol within 700 m to join in.

RED FLARE

Lose 40% of that squad within two minutes and a red flare goes up: 30% of the garrison and half of the roaming squads within 2.5 km come back to defend.

ROOT DEFENCE

Attack the first HQ, the root of the network, and every HQ it founded sends troops back to save it.

03
Air intelligence

MAGPIE hunts from the sky

MAGPIE is the friendly drone operator, a bot. She launches as soon as the carrier shows up, flies twelve drones at once, and only speaks when she has tracked something.

3 hunters

Darter, Greyhawk, Reaper

Flying at 250, 900 and 1,500 m. The higher the drone, the wider its sensor, up to 2.4 km. Fixed wings take off one at a time from the runway.

9 Darters

Scan chain

A north–south line of small drones sweeps east across the island, following the coast and never searching the sea.

First contact

Encirclement

The first enemy spotted becomes the centre of every flight path. The drones spread around it and tighten the ring, taking risks to see more.

Identification

Not every pass counts

Each pass has a chance to identify: 60% for infantry up to 90% for aircraft. Hidden units stay hidden.

Threat

Tigris

Every insertion drops a Tigris. Its radar only wakes once it notices a drone, missiles reach 3.5 km, and half of them are guided home. A lost drone is replaced after 8 minutes.

Map

Live and heat

04
Win and lose

Victory is total, defeat is one man

An HQ falls

Only when its commander is dead or captured and every installation is destroyed: depot, fuel, radio tower, helipad and AA.

Capture

Hold the action on the commander. He talks, and every HQ in his network goes on your map.

Victory

The carrier is empty and every enemy HQ on Altis is destroyed.

Defeat

Colonel Harlan, your commander in the HQ offices, is killed. The operation is over.

05
Your base

An airfield compound and its motor pool

Built by script at the AAC airfield from zones drawn on the map: a walled HQ, four vehicle rows, crate dispensers, repair depots facing the runway, a vehicle shop and a field of drone pads.

HQ compound

H-barrier walls, a gate between two towers, guard towers on the corners. Inside: offices, warehouse, radar, medical post, and one arsenal box per squad. Classes can only be changed inside these walls.

Vehicle rows

Four parallel rows, smallest vehicle first, spaced by each model's width plus 5 m, ending at a repair depot. Every row gets a similar mix.

Crates and loadout

Dispensers hold ready-filled supply crates; one action fills a vehicle with resupply for a whole squad.

Runway and defence

The runway is kept clear for MAGPIE. AA and HMG positions around the perimeter are rebuilt 10 minutes after they fall.

07
Radio and story

Three voices, no coordinates

Messages never give a location. The story follows the carrier: four acts as it empties, a SITREP every 20 minutes, and a speech budget so the net never floods. Serious milsim, with the dry humour of an old staff officer.

09
Under the hood

One director, two files

Vanilla + CBA

Base-game assets only. Every class is checked for DLC at runtime, with a base-game fallback.

One director

A single server loop with self-timed tasks: HQs, logistics, air, MAGPIE, balancing, crates, repair, stats.

Debug panel

F3 or the U menu, for the host and admins: seven categories of test commands, map overlays and a final player view.

Tunable

Timings, limits, prices, layout and the whole radio script live in cfg/settings.sqf and cfg/story.sqf.
