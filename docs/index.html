01
The enemy

A network that grows on its own

Nothing is placed by hand. A server-side director runs the enemy as a living system: it inserts, builds, resupplies, redeploys and calls for help every few seconds, whether you are watching or not. The carrier holds enough for six HQs, and its marker shows what is left.

Step 1

Insertion

Transport helicopters leave the carrier, wherever it parked this time: sixteen positions, never the same twice in a row. The first HQ goes inland of it, and a failed landing is simply retried a few minutes later.

Step 2

Build truck

A Zamak comes down under its own chute with the builders. It drives its squad to each building site, stops 20 m short, and the stage only goes up once it is there. A Kajman scouts 100–500 m around it. Kill the truck and building freezes until the carrier drops another, with one more Ifrit escort each time (up to 3).

Step 3

Secure

Squads post out to a 1 km perimeter before anyone lays a sandbag.

Then

Split

A strong HQ sends its build truck and colonists to found the next one and gets a new truck from the carrier, until the carrier is empty.

Stages 1–2

Defences first

Three AA pits, then a cargo tower with a heavy machine gun on top.

Stage 3

Radio tower

The HQ's voice, with a guard squad of its own. It can't be blown up: anyone hacks it from the terminal at its foot, an Engineer twice as fast.

Stages 4–5

Depot and fuel

A supply depot with a field supply truck, then a fuel tanker, each guarded by a team left behind by the truck squad. The depot only goes down to satchels or demolition charges.

Stage 6/6

Operational

Helipad with a QRF helicopter, a command building and a sandbag ring. The commander moves in: kill or capture him.

Logistics

The main HQ is resupplied by air and HEMTT convoys carry ammunition on to the others. A dead convoy is felt across the island.

Redeployment

Every minute the director evens out infantry across built HQs, by truck or helicopter. At most 160 enemy soldiers are on the ground at once.

Movement

Ifrits, Hunters and armour roam between HQs and avoid hilltops. Orca and Kajman helicopters fly irregular patrols.

Support teams

A medic team and an engineer team keep the network running. Each comes back three times, then it is gone for good.

Reinforcements

Weakened HQs call fresh sections from the carrier, while it still has men to send.

Rule

No markers

Enemy positions never appear on your map by themselves. Drones, the direction finder or a talkative commander put them there.

02
Contact

Get spotted and it escalates

Each HQ reacts to contact on its own, in steps. How fast you kill the squad that spotted you decides how bad it gets.

CONTACT

The spotting squad engages and calls every patrol within 700 m to join in.

RED FLARE

Lose 40% of that squad within two minutes and a red flare goes up: 30% of the garrison and half of the roaming squads within 2.5 km come back to defend.

ROOT DEFENCE

Attack the first HQ, the root of the network, and every HQ it founded sends troops back to save it.

03
Events

The island doesn't wait for you

Two things happen outside the HQ cycle. One guards the enemy's back from the start; the other falls out of the sky when nobody expects it and pulls both sides to the same spot.

From the start

Corridor outpost

On the highest ground between the carrier and the first HQ, guarding its flight path: a watchtower whose spotter calls in a mortar, two Titan AA, two HMG towers, a dug-in squad and two gunboats patrolling the coast. Its men share everything they see by radio.

Jammer

A rough idea, at best

A jamming mast blinds MAGPIE within 600 m and keeps the outpost off your map: you only get a hatched black circle about a kilometre wide. Destroy the mast, or crack a crash crate, and the marker becomes exact.

Reward

Cut the corridor

Take the outpost and the first HQ's resupply flights come late and the long way round, over 4 km off their line. The outpost doesn't count toward the enemy caps.

Random

Transport crash

An enemy transport goes down between your base and their network, carrying a communications crate. Its survivors dig in around it. 10% chance in the first 15 minutes, then whenever the front stalls, at most once every 40 minutes.

Clock

Hidden ten minutes

Once the survivors make contact, a clock you can't see starts. Run out of time and a CH-49 paradrops a Nyx, Kamysh, Marid or Gorgon full of soldiers that roam the site. The signal finder warns: ENEMY SECURING DEVICE.

Payoff

Decrypt

Reach the crate and hold the action: twelve minutes of decryption at base, and the network's secrets go on your map, the outpost's exact position included. The enemy can never take the crate back.

04
Air intelligence

MAGPIE hunts from the sky

MAGPIE is the friendly drone operator, a bot. She launches as soon as the carrier shows up, keeps two large Sentinels in the air, and has small Darters sleeping across the island. Her drones only report while a data link chains them back to the base. She only speaks when she has tracked something.

2 Sentinels

Argos and Hermes

Unarmed Greyhawk airframes at 900 and 1,500 m. The higher the drone, the wider its sensor. They take off one at a time from the runway.

Sleeping Darters

Wake on contact

A grid of points every 500 m across Altis. A ground enemy within 500 m wakes a Darter there; it looks, then backs off to the edge of its sensor. Lost ones are replaced from the nearest point a minute later.

First contact

Encirclement

The first enemy spotted becomes the centre of every flight path. The drones spread around it and tighten the ring, taking risks to see more.

Identification

Not every pass counts

Each pass has a chance to identify: 60% for infantry up to 90% for aircraft. Hidden units stay hidden.

Threat

Tigris

Every insertion drops a Tigris, and the corridor outpost has Titans. The radar only wakes once it notices a drone, and missiles reach 3.5 km. A lost Sentinel is replaced after 8 minutes; MAGPIE learns to fly around known AA.

Network

Data link

A drone reports only while it is chained to the base through overlapping ranges: base 5 km, hacked-tower relays 4 km, the relay vehicle 2.5 km, any friendly vehicle (even empty) 1 km, drones 1 km. Base and relays also link directly up to 10 km. Links are drawn on the map, and a Darter shows only while linked.

Relay vehicle

Hunter on the road

As soon as the first HQ exists, a crewed Hunter HMG drives a third of the way towards it and parks as a relay. It fires back, radios when it comes under fire, and is replaced 5 minutes after it is lost.

Map

Live and heat

While a drone sees an enemy, everyone sees it live on the map. A heatmap marks scanned ground in teal and contacts in rose, fading within a minute. heatmap in test

05
Win and lose

Victory is total, defeat is one man

An HQ falls

When its commander is dead or captured and its radio tower is hacked or gone. A hacked tower turns into your relay on the spot. Whatever is left falls back to the nearest HQ, its helicopters re-base, and a minute later its depot and tanker are blown up.

Capture

Hold the action on the commander. He talks, and every HQ in his network goes on your map.

Victory

The carrier is empty and every enemy HQ on Altis is destroyed.

Defeat

Colonel Harlan, your commander in the HQ offices, is killed. The operation is over.

06
Your base

An airfield compound and its motor pool

Built by script at the AAC airfield from zones drawn on the map: a walled HQ, four vehicle rows, four repair depots around the runway, a vehicle shop and a field of drone pads.

HQ compound

H-barrier walls, a gate between two towers, guard towers on the corners. Inside: offices, warehouse, radar, medical post, and one arsenal box per squad. Classes can only be changed inside these walls. A squad respawn vehicle can carry the spawn point to the front; while it does, it is locked, never despawns and pitches a camp: camo net, tent and campfire. Captured enemy vehicles can't be used.

Vehicle rows

Four parallel rows, smallest vehicle first, spaced by each model's width plus 5 m, ending at a repair depot. Every row gets a similar mix. Two more repair depots face them across the northern runway.

Autofill

Park on a depot helipad and pick Autofill: the cargo is loaded with the magazines and rockets your squad's own weapons use, balanced and scaled to the space. 40 s cooldown.

Runway and defence

The runway is kept clear for MAGPIE. AA and HMG positions around the perimeter are rebuilt 10 minutes after they fall. Light towers, a Hesco watchtower and a crewed GMG cover the north side.

Squads

Create, join, leave or disband. A leader invites an unassigned player, who answers in an accept or decline window.

Classes

Shown as used / limit. Each class starts with the best vanilla kit for its role and a suppressor, kept through respawns. The arsenal is role-aware: only medics take medikits, only launcher classes take launchers, but anyone can carry magazines and rockets for the squad. No personal drones.

Earplugs

On the scroll menu and the End key. One press brings the noise of battle down to a whisper; press again to take them out.

Stats

Every player's combat is tracked for the session: filter by class, sort by any column, open a player's details.

Tablet

Squads, classes and stats open on one field tablet in the middle of the screen. The U menu itself is a radio transceiver at the right edge.

Sandbags

Each Engineer carries two short sandbag walls: ghost preview, rotate, six seconds to build, anywhere outside the base. Indestructible, and recalled from the base to build them again.

08
Radio and story

Three voices, no coordinates

Messages never give a location. The story follows the carrier: four acts as it empties, a SITREP every 20 minutes, and a speech budget so the net never floods. Serious milsim, with the dry humour of an old staff officer.

10
Under the hood

One director, two files

Vanilla + CBA

Base-game assets only. Every class is checked for DLC at runtime, with a base-game fallback.

One director

A single server loop with self-timed tasks: HQs, build trucks, logistics, air, MAGPIE and its data link, balancing, repair, stats.

Debug panel

F3 or the U menu, for the host and admins. Docked on the right: teleporters first, then map visibility, HQs, forces, events, friendly, story and logs.

Tunable

Timings, limits, prices, layout and the whole radio script live in cfg/settings.sqf and cfg/story.sqf.
